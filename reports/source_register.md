## Interpretation rules

- Prefer official dataset definitions over terminology in third-party model papers.
- Publication dates and URLs establish identity, not methodological quality.
- Abstract-level entries cannot establish that a paper omitted a particular method.
- Search coverage is targeted, not exhaustive; do not claim absence of prior work.
- The bibliography uses verified details and shortened `and others` author lists where appropriate, rather than fabricated names or page ranges.
